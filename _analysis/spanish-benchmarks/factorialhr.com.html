Check out some of the most frequently asked questions about our business management software (BMS)

What is a business management software (BMS)?

A business management software (BMS) is a comprehensive set of tools to help businesses run and manage their daily operations. This includes features such as talent management, tracking project expenses, running payroll, and conducting performance reviews. Ultimately, a BMS brings all aspects of business management in one place so companies achieve their goals more easily.

What does Factorial software do?

Factorial is the business management software that connects all you need to manage your team. It automates repetitive tasks, from hiring and holidays to performance reviews and payroll, saving managers time to focus on their people. The easy-to-use platform unifies processes but decentralizes tasks so every team member has the power to participate. It gives leaders visibility over time, projects, goals, and spending to make data-driven decisions that grow their company and the people in it.

What is the purpose of an all in one business software?

The purpose of an all-in-one business software is to automate and centralize administrative tasks, giving people the headspace to work on strategic actions and managers the time to focus on their people. It connects all business operations, including tracking hours, managing absences, and monitoring projects in one easy-to-use platform.

Why do businesses need a BMS?

Utilizing a BMS allows businesses to manage and automate their core operations. It connects finance, HR, and analytics into one cohesive platform. It enables organizations to scale their teams by streamlining their workflows. It also provides managers with real-time insights to make better, data-driven decisions. This way, teams can spend less time searching for insights and more time acting on them.

How much does Factorial cost?

Pricing starts at $8 per employee per month (PEPM). Factorial follows a modular pricing package, which allows you to only pay for what you need. Review our pricing page for more information. We even offer personalized quotes for your team, inquire about one here.

What are the main features of business management software?

The main features of a business management software (BMS) are an employee platform, automation tools for operations, people management tools for employee development, and finance tools to help drive growth and keep companies compliant with employment laws. These features make strategic planning and operating a business easier.

What tasks can I automate with Factorial?

While using Factorial, you’ll be able to automate all tasks related to business management. From time management (clocking in, sick leave, PTO requests), talent management (onboarding, performance reviews, training courses), and finance management (payroll management, expense management, financial reporting).
